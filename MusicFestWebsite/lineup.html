Violent Vira

Name: Natalie Lu
Music genre: Nu gaze and alternative rock


Violent Vira is a rising American band focusing on the alternative rock and metal scene. The band was formed by Vira, a Mexican-American vocalist and songwriter, who later formed a band with bassist Blank and guitarist Matt and Anthony. Their lyrics often explore themes such as grief and religion with melodious sound!

Paramore

Name: Hayley Williams, Taylor York, Zac Farro
Music genre: Pop-punk, emo, and alternative rock


Paramore is an American rock band formed in Frankling, Tennessee, in 2004. The band is fronted by the vocalist Hayley Williams alongside core members such as the dummer Zac Farro, and guitarist Taylor York. Known for their blend of pop-punk, emo, and alternative rock, they rose to global fame with albums like "Riot!"(2007) which earned them multiple Grammy awards!

The Cranberries

Name: Dolores O'Riordan, Noel Hogan, Mike Hogan, Fergal Lawle
Music genre: Alternative rock, and indie rock


The Cranberries are an iconic Irish rock band from Limerick, formed in 1989. They are best known for their unique blend of alternative rock with elements of irish folk and dream pop. The band was fronted by the vocalist Dolores O'Riordan, alongside drummer Regal Lawler, guitarist Noel Hogan, and bassist Mike Hogan.

Deftones

Name: Chino Moreno, Stephen Carpenter, Abe Cunningham, Frank Delgado, Sergio Vega
Music Genre: Alternative metal, art rock, alternative rock, and shoegaze


Deftones is an influential American alternative metal band formed in Sacramento, California, in 1988 with childhood friends vocalist Chino Moreno, guitarist Stephen Carpenter, and drummist Abe Cunningham, later joined by turntablist Frank Delgado. They are famous for their signature sound, which blends heavy, aggressive riffs with atmospheric, dream-like soundscapes!

Halocene

Name: Addie Nicole Amick, Bradley Amick
Music genre: Alternative rock, pop-punk, and metal


Halocene is an American rock and metal band, primarily consisting of the husband-and-wife duo vocalist Addie Nicole Amick and guitarist Bradley Amick. Formed in Pheonix, AZ, in 2008, the band is well-known for their viral, high-energy covers as well as their incredible original music shared across social media!

Wisp

Name: Natalie Lu
Music genre: Nu gaze and alternative rock


Wisp is an American rock musician, singer, songwriter and guitarist who was raised in San Fransisco. She began releasing music in 2023 and quickly went viral for her ethereal shoegaze and rock music.
